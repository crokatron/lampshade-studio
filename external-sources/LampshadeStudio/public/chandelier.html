03 / COMPOSE IN SPACE

Spacing & movement

A single hanger on the hub. Bolted spokes keep the lights apart.

Spoke spacing & optional hub

The inner ends of the T5s are held by two collars beneath the hub; the lights themselves form the spokes. Finish the fixing and retention details in CAD. Fasteners and T5 clips are separate hardware. The bar housing and root joints have not been load-tested.

Keep room for the plugs. Bar dimensions stay fixed; spacing changes the overall envelope.

04 / CONNECT THE SPOKES

Curves between the lights.

One identical connector per gap, patterned around the lights. Integral clips are offset along each T5 so neighbouring copies can share the light. Print its broad face down with the clip openings up; test fit and retention on your actual bar.

05 / SUSPEND IT

Paracord or webbing.

Connector eyelets lead to one hanging point. The centre hub is optional; repeated connectors retain the lights around an open centre. Both versions need physical joint and load testing.

Flat strips with end holes, intended for flexible printing. The assembly shows straight spans; finish end fastenings and threading through the saddle slots in CAD. Material and print settings determine strength.

Saddle & clip fitting

Integral bridge clips include retaining lips. Check their clearance and flex on your actual light. The older separate collars use supplied metal clips and fasteners through their centre holes. Refine clip fit, edge radii and load-bearing details in CAD before making a suspended fixture.

Simple planes and cylindrical holes keep the components easy to edit in CAD.

06 / TAKE IT INTO CAD

Build around your lights.

STEP keeps a nested assembly of T5 lights, repeated clip bridges, optional hub and suspension parts. Repeated parts share geometry and part numbers. Cord holes and strap slots are real cut-outs. Paracord lines and the ceiling plate are preview guides and excluded from export.

T5 bodies are rectangular size references, not manufacturer CAD. Single-part STL exports are also available; mounting parts require normal solid printing, not vase mode. The flat strap export uses the longest span; each assembly strap matches its own span.

07 / PRINT VOLUME

Each part, checked.

Checks individual parts in their export orientation. Leave room for any brim. Oversized single-part STL exports are blocked; STEP remains available for splitting parts in CAD. The real light dimensions stay fixed.
